Quick Tips

🧥

Dress in Layers

SF weather changes throughout the day. Bring a jacket even in summer — the famous fog keeps things cool.

🚶

Wear Comfy Shoes

The city is hilly! Comfortable walking shoes are essential for exploring neighborhoods on foot.

🎫

Get a CityPASS

Save up to 46% on top attractions with a CityPASS — includes Alcatraz, aquarium, and more.

📱

Use Transit Apps

Download the MuniMobile app for easy public transit payments and real-time arrival info.

Getting Around San Francisco

Public Transit

BART (Bay Area Rapid Transit)

Fast rail system connecting SF to the airport (SFO), Oakland, and East Bay. Best for airport transfers and cross-bay travel.

Cost: $2.50 – $12.00 Hours: 5 AM – Midnight
Public Transit

Muni Bus & Metro

The city's bus and light-rail network covering all neighborhoods. Use the MuniMobile app for easy payment.

Cost: $2.50 per ride Pass: $5/day, $21/week
Scenic

Cable Cars

An iconic SF experience! Three lines run through the city. The Powell-Hyde line offers the best views. Expect lines at popular stops.

Cost: $8 per ride Tip: Board at less busy stops
Rideshare

Uber / Lyft

Widely available throughout the city. Convenient for late-night travel or reaching areas with limited transit.

Cost: $10 – $30 typical Tip: Surge pricing at peak hours
Rental

Bike Rentals (Bay Wheels)

Perfect for biking across the Golden Gate Bridge. Docking stations throughout the city. E-bikes also available.

Cost: $3.50/trip or $25/day Best for: Golden Gate Bridge ride
Scenic

Ferry (Golden Gate / Bay)

Scenic ferry rides to Sausalito, Tiburon, and Angel Island. Beautiful views of the skyline and Golden Gate Bridge.

Cost: $8 – $14 Departs: Ferry Building
Rental

Car Rental

Not recommended for downtown but useful for day trips to Napa Valley, Muir Woods, or along the coast.

Cost: $40 – $100/day Tip: Parking is expensive downtown
Scenic

Historic Streetcars (F-Line)

Vintage streetcars running along Market Street to Fisherman's Wharf. A fun and practical way to travel the waterfront.

Cost: $2.50 Route: Castro to Fisherman's Wharf

Food & Dining

Must Try

Clam Chowder in Sourdough Bowl

The quintessential SF food experience. Head to Boudin Bakery at Fisherman's Wharf for the original sourdough bread bowl.

Where: Fisherman's Wharf Price: $12 – $18
Must Try

Mission-Style Burrito

Giant, foil-wrapped burritos from the Mission District. La Taqueria and El Farolito are legendary spots.

Where: Mission District Price: $10 – $15
Must Try

Dim Sum in Chinatown

SF has the oldest Chinatown in North America. Try dim sum at City View or Good Mong Kok Bakery for fresh BBQ pork buns.

Where: Chinatown Price: $15 – $30
Fine Dining

Waterbar

Stunning waterfront seafood restaurant with views of the Bay Bridge. Excellent raw bar and sustainable seafood.

Where: Embarcadero Price: $60 – $120
Budget

In-N-Out Burger

The iconic West Coast burger chain. The Fisherman's Wharf location has waterfront seating with Golden Gate views.

Where: Fisherman's Wharf Price: $5 – $10
Market

Ferry Building Marketplace

A foodie paradise with artisan vendors, cafes, and a Saturday farmers market. Try Blue Bottle Coffee and Cowgirl Creamery.

Where: Embarcadero Price: $10 – $25
Must Try

Ghirardelli Chocolate

Visit the original Ghirardelli Square for free chocolate samples, hot fudge sundaes, and the famous chocolate shop.

Where: Ghirardelli Square Price: $8 – $15
Fine Dining

Hog Island Oyster Co.

Fresh, sustainably farmed oysters at the Ferry Building. Pair with a crisp white wine and bay views.

Where: Ferry Building Price: $30 – $60
Budget

Tartine Bakery

World-famous bakery in the Mission. The morning bun and country bread are legendary. Arrive early to beat the line.

Where: Mission District Price: $5 – $12

Sightseeing & Attractions

Landmark

Golden Gate Bridge

Walk or bike across the iconic 1.7-mile suspension bridge. Visit Battery Spencer on the Marin side for the best photo spot.

Cost: Free Time: 1 – 2 hours
Landmark

Alcatraz Island

Tour the infamous former prison on an island in the bay. The audio tour is excellent. Book tickets well in advance!

Cost: $42 (day) / $52 (night) Time: 2.5 – 3 hours
Nature

Golden Gate Park

A 1,000+ acre urban park with gardens, museums, lakes, and bison paddock. Bigger than Central Park!

Cost: Free (park) Time: Half day
Culture

Chinatown

The oldest Chinatown in North America. Enter through the Dragon Gate on Grant Avenue. Explore temples, shops, and tea houses.

Cost: Free to explore Time: 2 – 3 hours
Landmark

Fisherman's Wharf & Pier 39

Watch the sea lions, browse shops, and enjoy street performers. Touristy but a must-see for first-time visitors.

Cost: Free to explore Time: 2 – 3 hours
Neighborhood

Painted Ladies & Alamo Square

The famous row of colorful Victorian houses with the city skyline behind them. A postcard-perfect photo opportunity.

Cost: Free Time: 30 min – 1 hour
Nature

Muir Woods (Day Trip)

Ancient coastal redwood forest just 30 minutes north. Reserve parking or take the shuttle. An awe-inspiring experience.

Cost: $15 entrance Time: Half day
Culture

SF Museum of Modern Art (SFMOMA)

World-class modern art museum with works by Warhol, Kahlo, Pollock, and more. Don't miss the living wall.

Cost: $25 Time: 2 – 3 hours
Neighborhood

Lombard Street

The "crookedest street in the world" with eight tight hairpin turns. Walk down for a unique experience and great views.

Cost: Free Time: 30 min
Nature

Twin Peaks

Two 922-foot peaks offering 360° panoramic views of the entire city. Best at sunset or on a clear day.

Cost: Free Time: 1 hour
Culture

Mission District Murals

Explore vibrant street art in Balmy Alley and Clarion Alley. A colorful, free outdoor gallery showcasing local artists.

Cost: Free Time: 1 – 2 hours
Landmark

Palace of Fine Arts

A stunning Greco-Roman rotunda and colonnade originally built for the 1915 World's Fair. Beautiful at sunset.

Cost: Free Time: 1 hour
